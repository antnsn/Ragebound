# Explanations

Hover over a Ragebound card or the Hearthstone row of the travel panel and the tooltip says, in a sentence or two, what it is for. Turn it off once you know it all.

## What it does

The explanations show in the tooltips of:

- the [Battle Shout card](battle-shout.html), also when it offers a cast (the tooltip then names the spell and ends with **Click to cast (out of combat)**),
- the [durability card](durability.html),
- the [weapon stone card](weapon-stone.html), also when it offers a click (the tooltip then names the stone and ends with **Click to cast (out of combat)**),
- the [bandages card](bandages.html),
- the [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html) cards,
- the Hearthstone row of the [travel panel](travel.html).

The texts:

| Where | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Battle Shout card | Battle Shout raises the attack power of you and your party for 2 minutes. It costs 10 rage, so out of combat you may need to build rage first. |
| Durability card | Gear loses durability when you take hits, deal damage or die. A broken item (0%) gives none of its stats. Any blacksmith or armor and weapon vendor repairs it. |
| Weapon stone card | A sharpening stone (axes, swords, daggers, polearms, fist weapons) or weighted stone (maces and staves) adds damage to your main-hand weapon for 30 minutes. Blacksmiths make them; the Auction House sells them. |
| Bandages card | A bandage heals you over a few seconds, out of combat or in a pause in the fight. Damage interrupts it. You make bandages with First Aid from cloth. |
| Well-Rested card | Sleeping in a Cozy Sleeping Bag gives Well-Rested: more experience for a while. |
| Well Fed card | Eating buff food gives Well Fed: better stats, and on Forever more experience too. |
| Hearthstone (travel panel) | Your Hearthstone takes you back to the inn you set it to. Talk to an innkeeper to change it. |

## How to use it

Hover over a card or the row. That's all.

## Settings

In `/rage`, page **Skin**, section **General**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explain things in tooltips | On | Shows the explanations. Off: no explanations; a card that offers a click still shows what it casts and "Click to cast", and the Hearthstone row still shows its name. |

`/rage explain` turns it on or off from chat.

## Good to know

**No tooltip while frames are unlocked.**
While you move frames, the cards don't show their explanations, so they don't get in the way.

**Hovering a card doesn't block clicks.**
The durability and bandages cards show their tooltip, but clicks go through them to the world behind.
