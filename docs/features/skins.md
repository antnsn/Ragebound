# Skins

Ragebound comes in three looks. They all work the same way, only the frames look different, so you can match the rest of your UI.

## The three skins

| Skin | Looks like | Good with |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Rage** (default) | Dark iron panels with a blood-red edge, a layer of smoke, rivets in the corners and a faint Ragebound crest behind each card. A card that needs you has a slow ember-red glow along its edge. | The default UI, or if you like the warrior look. |
| **Blizzard** | The game's own gray and silver tooltips, dialogs and buttons. No crest on the cards. | A mostly default UI. |
| **Flat** | Thin black edges on dark gray, plain text, no crest. | EllesmereUI, ElvUI and other flat UI packs. |

## How to switch

![The Skin page]({{ '/screenshots/options-skin.png' | relative_url }})

1. Type `/rage` and open the **Skin** page.
2. Click the **Skin** button to step through the skins (right-click goes back).
3. Click **Reload now**, or type `/reload` later. The new skin shows after the reload.

The skin is saved with your other Ragebound settings.

## What changes, and what doesn't

A skin changes the frames: colors, borders, fonts, buttons and check boxes in the options window, the cards and the travel panel.

The warning color stands out in every skin: red for a missing Battle Shout, a broken item or no bandages left.

## Things to know

**Why does it need a reload?**
Ragebound builds its frames once, when you log in. Switching in the middle of a session would leave some frames in the old look and some in the new one, so the switch waits for the next reload.
