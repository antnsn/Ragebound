# Features

Ragebound does a few small warrior jobs for you. Each page below covers one of them: what it does, how to use it, the settings in the `/rage` window, and the things that surprise people. New to the addon? Start with the [Battle Shout card](battle-shout.html).

- [Battle Shout](battle-shout.html): a card when Battle Shout is missing or has 30 seconds left. Click to cast.
- [Gear durability](durability.html): a card when an equipped item is worn down, in red once something is broken.
- [Weapon stone](weapon-stone.html): a card when your main hand has no stone on it. Click to use your best one. Off by default.
- [Bandages](bandages.html): a card when you carry fewer than 5 bandages, once you know First Aid.
- [Buff key](buff-key.html): one key that does what a card asks for.
- [Travel panel](travel.html): your Hearthstone beside the game menu.
- [Well-Rested and Well Fed](well-rested-and-well-fed.html): a warning and a countdown before they run out.
- [Explanations](explanations.html): what each card and button does, in the tooltip.
- [Frames and minimap](frames-and-minimap.html): moving and resizing frames, the grid, and the minimap button.
- [Skins](skins.html): Rage, classic Blizzard or flat.
