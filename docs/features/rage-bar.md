# Rage bar

Your rage in a bar of its own, near your character instead of up in the corner. The fill shows how much you have, the number sits on it, and the bar lights up when you are at full rage.

## What it does

- **The bar.** A painted bar with an axe head on the end. The red fill grows with your rage, and a glowing line marks its end.
- **The number** of rage points sits on the bar. You can turn it off.
- **Full rage.** At 100 the whole bar glows: time to spend some.
- **When it shows.** While you have rage, and the whole time you are in combat. Out of combat with no rage, it hides. Turn on **Always show the bar** to keep it up.
- **Horizontal or vertical.** Vertical stands the bar up with the axe head at the bottom, and the fill grows upward.
- **Your size and place.** Unlock the frames to drag the bar where you want it, and scroll on it to make it bigger or smaller.
- **Skins.** In the Rage skin you get the painted bar. The Blizzard skin gives a plain bar with the game's bar texture, and the Flat skin a plain bar in flat colours.

## How to use it

1. Nothing to set up: the bar is on by default, below the middle of the screen.
2. Want it upright? Turn on **Vertical** on the **Rage bar** page.
3. Want it somewhere else? Click **Unlock frames** (or type `/rage unlock`), drag the bar, then lock the frames again.

## Settings

In `/rage`, page **Rage bar**:

| Setting | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Show the rage bar | On | Turns the bar on or off. |
| Vertical | Off | Stands the bar up, filling from the bottom. |
| Show the number | On | The rage points on the bar. |
| Always show the bar | Off | Keeps the bar up out of combat with no rage too. |

## Good to know

**The full glow doesn't show in combat.**
WoW: Forever hides some combat values from addons. If it hides your rage, the bar still fills and shows the number (the game draws them), but Ragebound can't tell when you are full.
