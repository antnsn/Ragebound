# Getting started
{: .no_toc }

From install to a warrior who never forgets Battle Shout.
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## 1. Install it

Pick one:

- **CurseForge app or WowUp:** search for **Ragebound** and click Install.
- **By hand:** download it from [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/ragebound) or from [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/antnsn/Ragebound/releases) (the `Ragebound-<version>.zip` file, not the green "Code" button). Unzip it into your `World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns` folder, so you get a folder called `Ragebound` there.

Start the game (or type `/reload` if it was already running) and log in on your warrior.

{: .note }
Ragebound only loads on warriors. On any other character it turns itself off for that character from the next login, and `/rage` only says that it does something on a warrior.

## 2. Open the options

Type `/rage` in chat, or click the Ragebound button on your minimap.

The options window has six pages on the left: **Buffs**, **Gear**, **Bandages**, **Travel**, **Extras** and **Skin**. Everything is on by default except the weapon stone card.

The default skin, Rage, is dark iron with a blood-red edge. See [Skins](features/skins.html).

## 3. Let the cards do their job

Play as usual. When Battle Shout has 30 seconds left, or is gone, a card shows up. Click it out of combat and Ragebound casts it. Battle Shout costs 10 rage, so out of combat you may need to build rage first. See [Battle Shout](features/battle-shout.html).

Out of combat, other cards show up when your gear is worn down or your bandages run low. See [Gear durability](features/durability.html) and [Bandages](features/bandages.html).

## 4. Turn on the weapon stone card

Optional. It is off by default.

1. In `/rage`, open the **Gear** page.
2. Tick **Show when your main hand has no stone**.
3. When your main-hand weapon has no stone on it, a card shows up. Click it out of combat to use your best stone on it.

More in [Weapon stone](features/weapon-stone.html).

## 5. Pick a buff key

Optional.

1. In `/rage`, open the **Extras** page.
2. Under **Buff key**, click the button next to **Key** and press the key you want.
3. Out of combat, press it. Each press does what a card asks for: Battle Shout first, then the weapon stone.

More in [Buff key](features/buff-key.html).

## 6. Put the frames where you want them

1. Type `/rage unlock`, Shift-click the minimap button, or click **Unlock frames** at the bottom of the options window.
2. Drag each frame where you want it. Scroll the mouse wheel over a frame to resize it.
3. Type `/rage lock` to lock them.

`/rage reset` puts every frame back where it started. More in [Frames and minimap](features/frames-and-minimap.html).

## That's it

Everything else works on its own. When you want to know what a card means or change how something behaves, look it up under [Features](features/).
