## Why Ragebound exists

A warrior has a short list of chores. Battle Shout lasts 2 minutes and drops all the time. Gear wears down until something breaks in the middle of a dungeon. Bandages run out right when you need one. A sharpening stone wears off after 30 minutes.

Ragebound watches all of that. It shows a card when Battle Shout is missing or about to run out, and you click the card to cast it. It warns you before your gear breaks and before your bandages run out. If you want, it also tells you when your weapon has no stone on it, and puts one on with a click. It only loads on warriors.

## What it does

- **Battle Shout.** A card when Battle Shout is missing or has 30 seconds left. Click it to cast. [More](features/battle-shout.html)
- **Gear durability.** A card when an equipped item drops to 25% or less, in red once something is broken. [More](features/durability.html)
- **Weapon stone.** Off by default. A card when your main hand has no stone on it. Click it to use your best sharpening or weighted stone. [More](features/weapon-stone.html)
- **Bandages.** A card when you carry fewer than 5 bandages, once you know First Aid. [More](features/bandages.html)
- **Rage bar.** Your rage in a bar of its own, horizontal or vertical, with a glow at full rage. [More](features/rage-bar.html)
- **Buff key.** Out of combat, each press does what a card asks for. [More](features/buff-key.html)
- **Travel panel.** Press Escape: your Hearthstone sits beside the game menu. [More](features/travel.html)
- **Well-Rested and Well Fed.** A warning and a countdown before they run out. [More](features/well-rested-and-well-fed.html)
- **Explanations.** Hover over a card or button to read what it does and why. [More](features/explanations.html)
- **Your layout.** Every frame can be moved, snapped to a grid and resized. [More](features/frames-and-minimap.html)
- **Three skins.** Rage, Blizzard and Flat. [More](features/skins.html)

## The Bound family

Ragebound is one of the Bound addons, one per class, built on the same core: Lightbound (paladin), Chronobound (mage), Stormbound (shaman), Packbound (hunter), Spiritbound (priest), Felbound (warlock), Dreambound (druid), Ragebound (warrior) and Shadowbound (rogue). They look like their class but work the same way, so if you know one, you know them all.
